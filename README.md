Colombian Payroll Overtime & Surcharge Calculator

This project is a Spring Boot application that calculates overtime payments and surcharges according to Colombian labor law.
It applies the official legal percentages for:

Daytime overtime (+25%)

Nighttime overtime (+75%)

Night surcharge (+35%)

Sunday/holiday surcharge (+75%)

Combinations of night, weekend, and holiday overtime.

Features:

Input: hourly wage or monthly salary, work schedule, and whether the day is a holiday.

Output: breakdown of normal hours, overtime, surcharges, and total payable amount.

Configurable rates in case of legal changes.

Disclaimer: This tool is for informational purposes only. Always verify results against official regulations or with a certified payroll professional.
